Why Revive?

Gamify Your Life

Stop treating personal growth like a chore. Revive transforms habits, fitness, learning, and projects into quests with real rewards. Level up your character as you level up yourself.

Train Real Skills

Pick from 11 skills across six attributes: Strength, Intelligence, Endurance, Wisdom, Charisma, and Luck. Each skill guides you on how to train through both insight (learning) and practice (doing).

Build Lasting Habits

Daily habits with streak tracking keep you accountable. Hit your goals and watch your XP climb. Your streak is your strength.
